# Combat on the Redmi K50 Ultra: black squares and fragment quad shuffles (2026-10-18)

中文：[2026-10-18-combat.zh-CN.md](2026-10-18-combat.zh-CN.md)

Measured 08:44–10:06 NZDT on the Redmi K50 Ultra (Adreno 730, Vulkan 1.1, Android 15). Build `android/linux-build`, with the Android defaults of [round 2](2026-10-17-perf-round2.md). The fix is PR #13 (branch `android/quad-derivatives`).

## Getting into a fight without playing

The saved Sorceress has only the camp waypoint. The host's own test switches, set in `files/dm/env`, take her into a fight in seconds:

| Line | Effect |
|---|---|
| `DM_TEST_WARP_LV=250:8` | at game tick 250 (10 s), sends the player into level 8, the Den of Evil (the game's own level change, as through a portal) |
| `DM_TEST_WORLD_SEEK=380:1:c19:L8` | from tick 380, jumps room by room to a unit of class 19 (Fallen) in level 8, then walks up to it |
| `DM_TEST_WORLD_LIST=450` | lists the loaded monsters and objects with their class ids and cells |
| `DM_GAME_SEED=12345` | the game's fixed-seed switch: the same level layout and monster packs every run, for before/after comparisons |

The fight itself is scripted Attack presses, with a potion every 15 s (the scratchpad `combat.sh`; it refuses to start above 36 °C). A level-1 Sorceress against a pack of Fallen and Fallen Shamans usually dies within a minute. That is fine for a test character.

## Black squares around units

In the first fight, blocky black squares covered the floor around the player, the monsters, the corpses and the dropped items. Units and item labels were still drawn inside them, and the black was sprinkled with white speckles. The cave before the fight and the camp were clean.

**Ruled out:**
- **Not the Android work.** With every newer engine option off by its marker (frame overlap, `MUTABLE_FORMAT`, persistent framebuffers, slot pools, texel memo, per-run queries, write tracking, in-place timestamps, native BC, the vertex cache: the old ~12 fps path) the squares stayed. They also stayed with all descriptor and binding caches off (`gfx-no-descmemo`, `-xmemo`, `-bindcache`, `-imgcache`, `-dynpartial`).
- **Not min / max with NaN.** The library uses `FMin`/`FMax`/`FClamp` (179,000 times, never the NaN-safe `NMin`/`NMax`/`NClamp`); rewriting them to the NaN-safe forms changed nothing.

**Cause:**
- The squares went away when the fragment shaders' `OpGroupNonUniformShuffle` was replaced by the lane's own value.
- 182 fragment shaders contain one such shuffle: the translation of Maxwell's `SHFL.BFLY` quad swizzle, `gl_SubgroupInvocationID ^ 2`, the vertically neighbouring pixel in the same 2×2 quad. In the main-pass shader it sits in the point-light branch, next to `dFdx`/`dFdy`.
- Vulkan does not promise that helper invocations (the pixels just outside a triangle that exist for derivatives) take part in subgroup operations. On Adreno they read garbage, and the NaNs spread through the blur chain.
- Units have many triangle edges, and in the Den torches and fire light them, which is why the squares followed units.

**Fix** (`dm/gfx/vulkan/engine/xbg_spirv_quad.cpp`): each float shuffle of a fragment module now takes the quad neighbour from fine derivatives, in which helper invocations always take part.
- Within a quad `dFdxFine(v)` = right − left, so the left pixel's neighbour is `v + d` and the right pixel's is `v − d`; the same applies vertically.
- The pixel's place in its quad comes from `gl_FragCoord`'s parity.
- All 182 shaders rewrite and pass `spirv-val --target-env vulkan1.1`.
- It is on by default on Android (`DM_GFX_QUAD_DERIVATIVES`); marker `gfx-quad-derivatives` = 0 turns it off.
- Later finding: all these shuffles sit in the translator's `ShuffleXor` helper, whose clamp check also returns lanes 32–63 of a 64-lane subgroup their own value. `main`'s guarded version reaches them at the call sites in PR #27; see [2026-10-19-spin-events-quad.md](2026-10-19-spin-events-quad.md).

## The fix is also the biggest GPU win so far

Back-to-back cold runs with the same APK, toggling only `gfx-quad-derivatives`:

| Scene | Fix off | Fix on |
|---|---|---|
| Camp, 3D scale held at 0.6, X2 pin | 44.7–44.9 fps, GPU 21.3 ms | **50.2–50.9 fps, GPU 17.6 ms** |
| Den fight, `DM_GAME_SEED=12345`, first 12 s, scale 0.75 | 38.8–41.0 fps, GPU 23.3–24.3 ms | **50.7–52.2 fps, GPU 18.0–18.5 ms** |

**Reading the numbers:**
- The fight's draw count differs by about 8% (1,100–1,137 vs 963–1,070), since combat does not replay exactly; the camp is the same scene.
- A subgroup shuffle in a fragment shader evidently costs Adreno much more than the single instruction it is.
- **With the Android defaults (dynamic 3D scale):** the camp now stays at scale 0.75 for about 90 s, then settles at 0.65, at 47–50 fps. Before the fix it settled on the 0.6 floor at about 45.5 fps.
- The camp at 0.6 is CPU bound again: submit 13.7 ms, GPU 17.6 ms.

## Other things the fights showed

- **Before the fix, the Den fight was GPU bound** at 36–37 fps (scale 0.75, GPU 25.8 ms), or 42 fps at 0.70 (GPU 22.7 ms), with about 1,100 draws. Per pass at 0.70:

  | Pass | Draws | µs |
  |---|---|---|
  | Main 3D | 312 | 10,614 (34 µs per draw, against 19 in the camp: many point lights) |
  | Shadow map | 204 | 2,852 |
  | Depth pre-pass | 312 | 2,266 |
  | Effects | 45 | 1,234 |
  | Light-culling compute (32×20 tiles) | — | 860 |

- **Hitches on first sight of new content:** 683 ms and 291 ms frames when the first pack came into view, almost all in submission (driver pipeline creation). Later runs did not repeat them, because the engine's warm-up list rebuilds the recorded pipelines at start. Rewritten shaders recompile once in that warm-up (the first frame took 1.5 s instead of 0.8 s).
- **Write-protect faults of the vertex/index cache:** about 160 per second while loading and in menus, 2–25 per second in the fight. The cache served 224–251 ranges (2.3–4.1 MB) per frame in the Den.
- **CPU in the fight:** submit 9–12 ms; the profile looks like the camp's (`memcpy` 12%, descriptors 5%).
